How RealityLens works

The classifier uses a real pretrained Vision Transformer executed in your browser by Transformers.js. Model files are downloaded from Hugging Face when first used, then normally cached by your browser.

Forensic views are deterministic pixel operations, not attention maps or evidence that pixels were AI-generated. The model was trained on CIFAKE and may not generalize to recent generators.

Video inference samples individual frames; it does not analyze audio or temporal deepfake cues. No automatic external fact-checking is performed.
